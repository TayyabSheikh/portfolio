Academic Spotlight

Master's Thesis Research

M.Sc. Thesis Project Submitted

Evaluating Hyper-spherical Embeddings and Targeted Augmentations for Medical Image Analysis

Otto-Friedrich-Universität Bamberg Bamberg, Germany • Department of Systems Science

Research Abstract: Medical image analysis presents severe challenges such as high data scarcity, label noise, and critical class imbalances. This research evaluates the efficacy of projecting deep visual representations onto high-dimensional hyper-spherical manifolds to enforce geometric constraints and improve representation learning. By combining hyper-spherical contrastive learning with targeted augmentations designed to mimic pathological variations, the proposed framework shows substantial improvements in robustness, out-of-distribution (OOD) generalization, and classifier confidence scores on complex medical diagnostic images.

Hyper-spherical Spaces

Investigated mathematical properties of deep embeddings mapped onto hyperspheres (Sd-1) to optimize inter-class distance and intra-class compactness.

Targeted Augmentations

Designed custom, pathology-preserving data augmentation techniques to boost generalization capabilities for rare diseases.

Clinical Imaging Datasets

Validated the models on major open-source medical imaging cohorts, examining reliability and classification confidence.

Deep Learning Contrastive Learning Hyper-spherical Embeddings Computer Vision PyTorch / TensorFlow Medical Diagnostics
